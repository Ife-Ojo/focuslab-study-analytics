# FocusLab - Data Collection Diary

I'm using this diary to keep track of how my data collection is going, any problems I run into and changes I make throughout the project.

## How I'm Collecting My Data

I'm collecting information about my own study sessions and storing it in `study_sessions.csv`.

For each session, I'm recording the module, location, activity, duration, interruptions, energy level, noise level, focus rating and whether I achieved my planned goal.

I'm using a timer to measure how long I study and counting interruptions that stop me working for at least 10 seconds. Energy, noise and focus are rated from 1 to 5.

I also created a separate file called `daily_log.csv` for days when I don't study at all. These days have zero sessions and zero minutes recorded. This means I can keep track of breaks in my study routine instead of having unexplained gaps between sessions.

## 26 October - Reading Week

My dataset starts on 26 October, which was during reading week.

Because I didn't have my usual lectures and commute, this period gave me more time for studying at home or in the library.

One thing I considered was whether to use fixed study blocks, but I decided against it because I don't normally study in fixed sessions. I could spend longer on a subject one session and then next session, spend a short amount of time on it, particularly when working through Maths questions or preparing for a CA.

Recording the actual duration should give me a better picture of my study habits.

## 29 October - Tracking Days Without Studying

I decided to add another CSV file specifically for days when I don't study.

Originally, I was only recording the sessions I completed, but I realised this wouldn't account for days when I didn't study at all.

Keeping these dates separately should help me analyse how consistent my study routine is and whether there are any patterns in the days I take off.

## Early November - Change in Routine

My data collection has been affected by an unexpected knee injury which resulted in me spending time in hospital.

Because of this, I haven't been following my normal college routine and my study opportunities have been more limited.

This is something I'll need to take into account when analysing the data because the hospital period isn't really representative of a normal college week.

Rather than treating days without studying as missing sessions, I'll record them separately in the daily log.

### Something I Want to Investigate

I think my total study time might increase again once I'm home and able to get back into a routine, especially as I'll have college work to catch up on.

This is only a prediction for now. Once I've collected more data, I can compare study time during this period with the following weeks and see whether that actually happens.

## Problems and Changes So Far

The main challenge has been keeping my data collection consistent when my routine changes.

During reading week, longer sessions were more practical because I had fewer college commitments. During normal college weeks, I'll have to fit studying around lectures and travelling.

The hospital stay has also shown me that there will be periods when I can't collect as much data as planned.

I've decided to keep track of these changes rather than expect every week to look the same. I think this will make the final analysis more useful because I'll be able to explain some of the differences in my results.
